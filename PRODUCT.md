# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Solo riders with one or more motorcycles. They log a fill-up on their phone right after paying at the pump (seconds, one hand), and check mileage, spend and trends occasionally, at home on phone or desktop.

## Product Purpose
Track fuel fill-ups per vehicle and derive mileage (km/l), cost per litre, distance and spend. Success: logging a fill-up is effortless, and the mileage number is trustworthy and instantly readable.

## Positioning
Mileage is computed full-tank-to-full-tank from odometer order, starting from the second entry. Fully offline-capable PWA; data per vehicle.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Vehicle switcher, add/import (CSV) entries, Overview / Entries / Stats / Trends, settings, auth. No currency symbol is assumed (amounts are unitless). Entries have date, odometer, station, litres, amount paid. Pending entry = latest, no mileage yet.

## Brand Commitments
None binding. User confirmed only content is fixed; name treatment, mark and color are open for the redesign.

## Evidence on Hand
Synthetic demo data only in prototypes; no real user data or testimonials.

## Product Principles
- Logging beats reporting: the add action is always one thumb away.
- One number leads: average mileage.
- Honest about missing data (pending, regressed odometer).
- Works on a phone in sunlight and on a desktop at night.
